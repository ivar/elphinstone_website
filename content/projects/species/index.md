Before changing a piece of land, it helps to know who already lives there. This list is our baseline: every species that has been photographed on the property and identified on [iNaturalist](https://www.inaturalist.org/), a community science platform where naturalists confirm each other's sightings.

Anyone visiting can add to it. Photograph what you find with the iNaturalist app while you're here, and once it is identified to species it will appear on this page.
