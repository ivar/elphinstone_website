The workspace is meant to sit close to the growing projects, so the people building tools and the people using them are never far apart.

## Appropriate technology

We favour {{< term "appropriate technology" >}}Tools designed to suit the people, place and scale where they're used — affordable, repairable and understandable, rather than complex for its own sake.{{< /term >}}: tools that serve the land and the people, not the other way around.

## The decentralized web

Many of the projects we want to host are {{< term "dweb" >}}The decentralized web: software that works peer-to-peer rather than relying on a single central server, so communities can own and keep their own data.{{< /term >}}-affiliated — software that lets small communities share information without depending on large platforms.
