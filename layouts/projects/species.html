How this list is made

Each time the site is published it collects the iNaturalist observations that fall inside the property boundary and are identified to species. Cultivated plants and captive animals are left out, as are observations whose location is hidden. Introduced marks species that iNaturalist lists as not native to British Columbia. Unconfirmed means no observation of that species has yet been confirmed by the iNaturalist community, so the identification may change. Tap a photo to see the original observation, or a name to learn about the species.
